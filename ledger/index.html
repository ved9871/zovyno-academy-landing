IXThe essentials

Questions, answered.

Clear expectations matter — especially when education touches trading, digital assets and future career pathways.

01Are courses currently available?

This is a design concept. The foundation courses and platform journeys displayed here are proposed; they should not be shown as open for enrollment until the Academy team has verified availability.

02Do I need to buy tokens or connect a wallet?

No. Token purchase, exchange deposit, wallet connection, node purchase or recruitment activity is not a prerequisite for exploring course information — or for beginning free foundation learning when it launches.

03Does ZOVYNO teach general artificial intelligence?

No. The curriculum does not include a generic AI school or AI programming. Only the safe use, evaluation and limitations of ORZENO AI-assisted trading product features may be covered within trading education.

04Is trading education investment advice?

No. Training is educational and risk-aware. Simulations are clearly labeled, no investment returns are promised, and actual trading may involve significant losses.

05Are certificates or leadership roles guaranteed?

No. Any future certificate must follow defined assessments and verification rules. Trainer status or leadership appointment would require separate evaluation and is never guaranteed by payment, referral activity or course completion alone.
